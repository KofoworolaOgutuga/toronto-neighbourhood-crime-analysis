# Data

This folder contains the datasets used for the Toronto neighbourhood crime analysis.

## Primary Data Sources

The project primarily uses public datasets from the City of Toronto Open Data Portal.

### Major Crime Indicators

Used as the primary dataset for analyzing reported crime incidents across Toronto.

https://open.toronto.ca/dataset/major-crime-indicators/

### Wellbeing Toronto: Safety

Used to provide additional neighbourhood-level safety and population context.

https://open.toronto.ca/dataset/wellbeing-toronto-safety/

---

## Data Used in the Analysis

The analysis focuses on information relevant to:

* Toronto neighbourhoods
* Reported crime
* Crime categories
* Population
* Crime rates
* Historical crime trends
* Community safety

The full datasets were not necessarily required for every part of the analysis. Relevant portions of the available data were selected and prepared based on the research questions.

---

## Data Preparation

Before analysis, the data was reviewed and prepared for use.

This included:

* Selecting relevant variables
* Reviewing neighbourhood names
* Organizing data for comparison
* Cleaning relevant records
* Preparing data for analysis and visualization
* Combining relevant information where appropriate

---

## Data Limitations

The data represents reported and recorded incidents and should not be interpreted as a complete measure of all crime.

Differences between neighbourhoods may also be influenced by:

* Population
* Daytime population
* Commercial activity
* Transportation
* Tourism
* Reporting behaviour
* Policing and enforcement
* Geographic differences

These factors should be considered when interpreting the results.
